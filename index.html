Frequently asked questions

How much does a proposal cost?

Concept notes start from K1,500, full proposals and business plans from K5,000, and premium packages from K12,000. After a free consultation you get a fixed quote, so there are no surprises.

How long does it take?

A concept note usually takes 3–5 working days, a full proposal or business plan 7–14 working days, and large or complex applications 2–4 weeks. Urgent deadlines can be discussed.

What information do I need to provide?

Basic details about your organisation, the project idea, where it will happen, who benefits, costs you already know, and the funder's guidelines if you have them. We send a simple checklist to guide you.

Do you guarantee funding?

No one can honestly guarantee funding, because the funder makes the final decision. We make sure your application is complete, clear, compliant and competitive.

Can you work with clients outside Lusaka or outside Zambia?

Yes. We work fully online by WhatsApp, email and video call, and meet in person where possible.

Is my project information kept confidential?

Yes. Your ideas, financial information and documents are used only to prepare your application and are not shared without your permission.
